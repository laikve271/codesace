Privacy.
Handled responsibly.

This concept privacy policy explains how CODES Air Conditioning & Electrical may collect, use and protect personal information through this website.

Information we may collect

When you contact CODES through the website, by phone or by email, information may include your name, contact details, address or project location, enquiry details and any other information you choose to provide.

How information may be used

Personal information may be used to respond to enquiries, prepare quotes, arrange site visits, provide services, communicate about projects, maintain business records and meet legal or regulatory obligations.

Website and third-party services

This website may use third-party services for hosting, forms, analytics, maps or embedded content. Those providers may process limited technical information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Disclosure

Personal information is not intended to be sold or rented. It may be disclosed where reasonably required to deliver services, to professional advisers or service providers, or where required by law.

Security

Reasonable steps should be taken to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure.

Access and correction

You may contact CODES if you wish to request access to, or correction of, personal information held about you.

Contact

For privacy enquiries, contact CODES Air Conditioning & Electrical at codes.aceservices@gmail.com or 0430 052 037.
