Air Conditioning Tradesperson - SA

At CODES Air Conditioning & Electrical, we take pride in delivering superior air conditioning services to our clients. With a strong commitment to excellence and customer satisfaction, we have built a strong reputation within the HVAC industry. As we continue to grow, we are looking for a skilled and dedicated Air Conditioning Technician to join our team.

Key Responsibilities

  • Installation of pipework on commercial projects, including 1:1, VRV, Heat Pump & Reclaim.
  • Install, maintain and repair air conditioning systems.
  • Perform diagnostic tests to identify issues and provide efficient solutions.
  • Collaborate with team members and communicate with clients to explain repairs and provide recommendations.
  • Keep accurate records of work performed and parts used.
  • Stay updated on industry trends and best practices.

Qualifications

  • Proven experience as an Air Conditioning/Refrigeration Technician.
  • ARCTICK certification and relevant trade licences/qualifications.
  • Strong knowledge of air conditioning systems, components and troubleshooting.
  • Skilled pipework installation, executed with faultless precision.
  • Excellent problem-solving skills and attention to detail.
  • Ability to work independently and in a team.
  • Excellent communication and customer service skills.
  • Construction White Card.
  • Valid driver's licence.

Benefits

  • Ongoing training and development opportunities.
  • Friendly and collaborative work environment.
  • Opportunities for career growth and advancement.
  • Team days.

Mech Elec Electrician - SA

We are a growing Air Conditioning and Electrical contracting company delivering commercial mechanical and electrical projects. We are looking for a competent Mechanical Electrical Electrician to join our team and work on a variety of commercial installations.

This role suits a tradesperson who is confident working with mechanical services electrical systems, understands project drawings and can work independently on commercial sites.

Key Responsibilities

  • Install and terminate electrical wiring for mechanical services equipment.
  • Understand and fault find existing mechanical equipment.
  • Work from electrical and mechanical drawings to complete installations.
  • Collaborate with team members and communicate with clients to explain repairs and provide recommendations.
  • Keep accurate records of work performed and parts used.
  • Stay updated on industry trends and best practices.
  • Wire and commission equipment including VRV/VRF units, packaged units, pumps and fans, control panels and air quality control.
  • Interpret control wiring diagrams and install associated field wiring.
  • Install containment systems including tray, conduit and cable supports.
  • Work alongside HVAC technicians and builders on commercial projects.
  • Maintain a high standard of workmanship and site safety.

Essential Skills & Experience

  • Qualified Electrician.
  • Experience in mechanical electrical / HVAC electrical systems.
  • Ability to read schematics, wiring diagrams and plans.
  • Experience wiring controls, motors and mechanical plant.
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a team.
  • Strong problem solving and attention to detail.
  • Construction White Card.
  • Current driver's licence.
  • Commercial construction experience.
  • Commissioning mechanical plant.
  • Experience working with BMS / control systems.

What We Offer

  • Stable, long-term work on commercial projects.
  • Supportive team environment.
  • Opportunity to grow with an expanding company.
  • Competitive pay based on experience.
  • Company vehicle.
